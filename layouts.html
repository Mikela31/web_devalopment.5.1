Iron Man

Anthony Edward "Tony" Stark is a fictional character and the main protagonist of the Marvel Cinematic Universe. He is a billionaire industrialist and genius inventor who uses his wealth and intelligence to become the superhero Iron Man.

Spider-Man

Peter Benjamin "Pete" Parker is a fictional character and the main protagonist of the Marvel Cinematic Universe. He is a brilliant scientist and skilled martial artist who uses his intelligence and abilities to become the superhero Spider-Man.

Thor

Thor Odinson is a fictional character and the main protagonist of the Marvel Cinematic Universe. He is a powerful deity from the planet Asgard who uses his strength and wisdom to become the superhero Thor.
